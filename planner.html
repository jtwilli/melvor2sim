Combat setup

For other gear, food or prayers, set them up in the combat simulator and save them to a loadout in game.
At every level the planner picks the fastest action, counting the time to gather and craft its ingredients from nothing. XP that other skills earn along the way raises their levels too, so later steps can use what they unlock. A focus limits the final action; ingredients still come from wherever is fastest.
